- No filler, no windup, no recap at the end.
- Try to stick to the source material. If you add something that isn't in the slides, say so (you are allowed to interpolate from a standard Swiss curriculum if the information is scarce).
- Match the slide language: German slides → German technical terms, even if the explanation is in English.
- Math in LaTeX.
